QuizHell

QuizHell is a web platform for creating and sharing quizzes that fight back. Creators build a quiz and share one public link, and players join with just a nickname, no account needed. While answering real questions, players face the Rage System: playful visual and audio pranks such as moving buttons, fake countdowns, and trap questions. The pranks never touch the real answers, score, or timer.

Key Highlights:

  • Rage System: Designed three difficulty levels (Mild, Annoying, Hell) that control how often pranks appear and which ones are in the pool, with a rule that prevents the same prank from repeating back to back.
  • Server-Authoritative Quiz Engine: The server handles timing, scoring, and deadlines. Answers are saved as players choose them, attempts can be resumed from the same link, and answer keys are never sent to the browser.
  • Creator Dashboard & Analytics: Built a quiz builder with Multiple Choice and True/False questions, publish/unpublish controls, a dashboard with status and Rage Level filters, and per-quiz analytics (average score, completion rate, hardest question).
  • Excel Export: Results can be exported as a formatted .xlsx workbook with a player results sheet and a summary sheet, generated without external libraries.
  • Security Basics: Password hashing, CSRF protection, rate limiting, prepared statements, and output escaping.

Tools used: PHP 8.1 (native, no framework), MySQL, JavaScript (ES modules), custom CSS, and PDO.

The Last Seed

The Last Seed is a post-apocalyptic visual novel made for GameSeed 2024. The world has been wrecked by nuclear war and ecological disaster. The protagonist, Timun Mas ("Timas"), and her friend Raya travel in search of the "Seed of Life", hidden in a mysterious place called AcT-0. Along the way, players face moral choices, mutant creatures, and abandoned cities, and their decisions shape the story and its ending.

Key Highlights:

  • Game Design: Designed the branching narrative and the survival elements, building choices with real consequences for the characters and the ending.
  • Game Programming: Supported the programming side, implementing story flow and choice logic in Ren'Py.
  • Narrative Theme: Combined survival, moral dilemmas, and ecological themes into a story-driven experience.

Tools used: Ren'Py (Python).
